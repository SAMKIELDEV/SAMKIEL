# PRD: SAMKIEL Company Website

## Project Overview
**Company:** SAMKIEL  
**Founder:** SAMKIEL  
**Location:** Ile Ife, Nigeria  
**Website:** samkiel.tech  
**Stack:** Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, Framer Motion

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This PRD covers the **company landing page** hosted at `samkiel.tech`. The goal of this page is to establish SAMKIEL's identity, communicate what it does, showcase its studio products, and convert visitors into clients or collaborators.

## 2. Core Identity & Vision
SAMKIEL is not just a software agency; it's a "digitalization" hub. The company operates two main arms:
1.  **SAMKIEL Agency:** Building bespoke solutions for clients.
2.  **SAMKIEL Studio:** Creating in-house products (SaaS, platforms, tools).

**Tagline:** "Digitalizing The World"

---

## 3. Target Audience
-   **Potential Clients:** Businesses looking for elite web/app development and digitalization.
-   **Users:** People looking for the Studio's proprietary tools.
-   **Collaborators/Talent:** Developers/Creatives looking to join a high-performance culture.

---

## 4. Key Sections & Features

### A. Hero Section
-   **Visual:** High-fidelity, premium dark aesthetic. Smooth entry animations.
-   **Copy:** Bold "SAMKIEL — Digitalizing The World."
-   **CTA:** "Get Started" (Scroll to Contact) or "View Studio."

### B. The Agency (Service Arm)
-   **Focus:** Problem-solving via software.
-   **Key Services:** Full-stack development, Digital Transformation, UI/UX Strategy.
-   **Aesthetic:** Professional, sharp, data-driven.

### C. The Studio (Product Arm)
Showcase in-house products. For now:
1.  **BreezrChat**
    - Description: Lightweight, lightning-fast chat solution.
    - Link: `breezrchat.samkiel.tech`
2.  **SKDL**
    - Description: Premium platform for digital asset management/access.
    - Link: `skdl.samkiel.tech`

### D. About Section
-   Brief story of the company’s "High Performance" and "No-BS" philosophy.
-   Emphasis on the Ile Ife base and global reach.

### E. Contact Section
-   Simple, sleek form (or direct email).
-   Social links: X (Twitter), LinkedIn, GitHub.

---

## 5. Visual Language & Style Guide
-   **Theme:** Dark mode by default (`#0A0A0A` background).
-   **Accent Colors:** 
    - Zinc/White (Primary text)
    - Lime/Accent: `#E8FF47` (Buttons/Links)
-   **Typography:** 
    - Display: `Syne` (Bold, modern)
    - Body: `Figtree` (Clean, readable)
-   **Feel:** High-fidelity, smooth, fast, "Apple-meets-Cyberpunk."

---

## 6. Technical Requirements
-   **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router).
-   **CSS:** Tailwind CSS 4 (using `@theme inline`).
-   **Animations:** Framer Motion (Scroll reveal, staggering).
-   **Analytics:** Vercel Analytics.
-   **SEO:** Metadata API for high searchability.
